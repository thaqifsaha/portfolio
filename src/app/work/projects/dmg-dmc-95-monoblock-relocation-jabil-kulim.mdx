## Machine relocation

Assisted in relocating a DMC 95 monoBlock CNC machine to create space for the installation of a new DMU 125 unit. The process involved safely disassembling the swivelling rotary table from the machine to reduce weight and ensure a safer and more controlled relocation.

This task required careful coordination, planning, and adherence to safety procedures during on-site operations.

## Follow-up: alignment & troubleshooting

After successfully relocating the DMC 95 monoBlock, an issue occurred where the swivelling rotary table became stuck during movement into the machine. Under the guidance of a senior engineer, I assisted in checking the machine and table geometry to ensure proper alignment.

Using levelling tools, adjustments were made to both the machine and the table to achieve accurate positioning and restore smooth operation.
